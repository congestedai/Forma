# Alien Forma themes

## Included themes

Orbital Light, Deep Void and Solar Storm are included in the application. Select them in the theme picker; no separate download is needed. Earlier releases display their Portuguese names: Claro orbital, Vazio profundo and Tempestade solar.

## Extra supporter pack

Titanium, Technical Paper, Nebula, Forest and High Contrast are available through [Alien Forma on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/FormaCAD), in the US$5 and US$40 tiers. These extras are not hosted in this public repository. The current pack uses Portuguese names.

1. Download and extract the ZIP attached to the Patreon post.
2. Open the application's theme library.
3. Choose **Import themes** (`Importar temas` in the current preview). Select `.forma-themes.json` for the whole pack or `.forma-theme.json` for one theme.
4. Choose **Use** (`Usar`) on your preferred theme.

Themes are small color files, with no images, plugins or executable code. They are stored in the local profile. **Export library** saves a backup or transfers them to another profile. Importing and using these files does not require a Patreon login inside the application. Properly obtained themes continue to work offline after cancelling support; access to new packs follows the selected tier's conditions.
